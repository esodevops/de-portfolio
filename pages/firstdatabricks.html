Project overview

Customer and order data, shaped for decisions.

The notebook combines customer profiles with order transactions in Databricks. It standardizes raw fields, engineers registration and order date features, explores geography and activity, then ranks customers by frequency, spend, and average order value.

Analytics focus

Questions that turn rows into context.

The analysis moves beyond simple totals to expose geographic patterns, customer lifecycle signals, order performance, and behavioral segments that can support marketing and operations.

01

Geographic reach

Identify the cities, states, and country combinations with the largest customer populations.

02

Customer activity

Compare active and inactive customer counts by state using grouped and pivoted results.

03

Registration recency

Use rank, dense rank, and row number to find the newest customers within each state.

04

Order performance

Measure purchase frequency, total spend, average order value, and revenue leaders.

05

Status and seasonality

Explore order lifecycle status and monthly volume to reveal operational patterns.

06

Behavioral segments

Surface frequent customers whose total spend remains relatively low for targeted analysis.

Data workflow

A focused Spark analytics pipeline.

Each stage is designed for notebook-based exploration in Databricks while producing reusable, columnar outputs for downstream work.

01

Ingest

Load customer and order CSV files from a Unity Catalog volume into Spark DataFrames.

02

Clean

Parse dates and replace missing city, state, and country values with a consistent fallback.

03

Engineer

Create year and month features that make registration and order timelines easier to analyze.

04

Explore

Aggregate geography and activity data, pivot status counts, and inspect customer distribution.

05

Rank

Apply Spark window functions within states and across customer purchasing metrics.

06

Join

Connect orders to customer profiles through the shared customer identifier.

07

Publish

Write processed customer datasets and recent-customer subsets to Parquet.
