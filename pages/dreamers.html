Project overview

From one retail export to a trusted data model.

Dreamers E-commerce begins with a denormalized CSV of invoices, customers, products, prices, quantities, dates, and countries. The pipeline separates those records into focused data assets, preserves their relationships, and prepares them for reporting in PostgreSQL.

Analytics focus

Questions that make commerce data useful.

Ten SQL queries turn the normalized model into a practical reporting layer for understanding customer reach, product value, order activity, sales volume, and revenue.

01

Customer reach

Count customers by country to reveal where the store's audience is concentrated.

02

Catalog pricing

Measure average product price and surface the ten most expensive catalog items.

03

Order activity

Identify the customers with the most invoices and understand repeat purchasing behavior.

04

Product demand

Aggregate quantities across invoice lines to rank the products selling in the highest volume.

05

Invoice value

Join products and invoice items to calculate and rank the store's highest-value invoices.

06

Operational totals

Track core counts for customers, products, invoices, and total quantity sold.

Data pipeline

A repeatable route from CSV to PostgreSQL.

The workflow keeps extraction, transformation, loading, orchestration, and analysis distinct so each responsibility remains understandable and reusable.

01

Extract

Read the raw e-commerce CSV from the configured source directory into Pandas.

02

Standardize

Parse invoice dates and assign consistent customer identifiers for dependable relationships.

03

Transform

Split the flat source into customers, products, invoices, and aggregated invoice items.

04

Persist

Write each processed dataset to CSV, creating a reusable intermediate data layer.

05

Load

Create the database schema and load the four related tables into PostgreSQL.

06

Orchestrate

Use Apache Airflow to define the ETL workflow as a schedulable data pipeline.

07

Analyze

Run SQL aggregations and joins that answer ten core retail business questions.

Database design

Four connected commerce tables.

The dreamers schema separates business entities while foreign keys connect every invoice to a customer and every line item to its invoice and product.

customersCustomer identity and country
productsStock code, description, and price
invoicesCustomer purchases and timestamps
invoice_itemsProduct quantities per invoice
